    How it works

    1. Measure structure firstLinks, IP URLs, shorteners, capitals, exclamations and sender domain — read from the raw text, before any cleaning destroys them.
    2. Vectorize the textTF-IDF over 5,000 unigrams and bigrams, fitted on the training split only.
    3. CombineStructural features scaled to [0,1] and stacked beside the TF-IDF block.
    4. Classify & explainLogistic Regression returns a probability; every signal that fired is listed back to you.

    A false positive withholds real mail — a job offer, a medical result. Treat a low-confidence PHISHING verdict as a warning banner, not a silent block.
